Both builds use the same overlay and the same Zephyr tree; the only difference
is lab03-task-c.patch. Figures as reported by the linker at the end of the build.

                  superloop      + sampling thread        delta
  FLASH           135524 B           135604 B            +80 B
  iram0_0_seg      38020 B            38720 B           +700 B
  dram0_0_seg      36104 B            38520 B          +2416 B

The 2416 B of RAM is what the first thread costs: 1536 B of stack
(SAMPLING_STACK), 32 B for the 8-deep queue of uint32_t release timestamps,
and the rest is the k_thread control block, the three atomics and alignment.
